National unsimplified river, stream, water and residential features are distributed in inputs/*.json.gz, with a catalogue and source coverage in inputs/manifest.json. Tile SHA-256 hashes describe the uncompressed JSON payload. Download links are listed in inputs/data.html.
The original pilot source features are distributed as narayani/source.geojson. The adapted polygon and scenario database is in narayani/manifest.json. Mapped features may be missing or outdated. Residential land use does not represent individual houses or population.
